Pool removal & landscaping

Remove the pool, Reclaim the space

An old or unused swimming pool can take up valuable backyard space while adding ongoing maintenance and limiting future renovations. Removing it gives you the freedom to create a lawn, garden, entertaining area or expand your next project.

Every pool is different, so the right removal approach depends on the pool type, site access, surrounding structures and your plans for the area. We assess each property individually to ensure the site is prepared properly for its next use.

Pool Backfilling

Pool decommissioning and backfilling can be a practical option when the goal is to reclaim the space for other uses.

Partial Pool Removal

Specific sections of the pool are demolished, with the remaining structure then prepared and backfilled. The intended use of the area should be taken into account when determining whether this approach is appropriate.

Complete Pool Removal

The entire pool structure is removed from the ground, along with the associated materials and debris. The area is then backfilled and prepared for its next use, providing a clean slate for future plans.

What’s Involved in Pool Removal

Removing the Pool Is Just the Start

Pool removal can involve much more than taking out the pool itself. Depending on the site, the work may include service disconnections, demolition, excavation, removal of concrete and steel, imported fill, compaction, drainage and preparation of the final ground levels.

  • Pool structure removal
  • Partial demolition and pool decommissioning
  • Suitable fill and controlled backfilling
  • Solutions for tight or restricted sites
  • Coordination of topsoil, turf, paving and landscaping

How the Process Works

Turning Unwanted Pools into Ready-to-Use Space

Every removal is planned around site access and future land use, matching the right demolition and fill technique to your property.

Site Assessment

Initial assessment covers the pool type, size, access, surrounding structures and future use of the space.

Scope & Quotation

The contractor establishes what is required for demolition, disposal, backfilling, drainage, approvals and site reinstatement.

Removal & Site Compaction

Once the agreed removal work is complete, the remaining void is prepared and backfilled.

Site Reinstatement & Finishing

The finished area can be graded and prepared for turf, landscaping or other planned uses.
